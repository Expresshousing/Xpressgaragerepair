## Functional limits

The form prepares an email in the visitor’s email app. It does not submit to a server or confirm an appointment. All three states use the existing published email inbox; state and regional phone are included in the review and email subject/body. No separate regional email addresses were invented. New Jersey shares the Pennsylvania phone because the original Camden page publishes that contact. State selection does not request precise geolocation.
